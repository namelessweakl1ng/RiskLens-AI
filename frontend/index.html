LEARNING CENTRE

Understand before you sign

Simple explainers for common agreement terms.

01

Know your total cost

Look beyond the headline rate. Check processing fees, penalties, taxes, insurance premiums and recurring charges.

Ask for a written breakdown showing every one-time and recurring cost over the full term.
02

Check what can change

Rates, fees, coverage and rules can sometimes change after you sign.

Find the change-notice period, the party who can change the terms, and your option to cancel.
03

Read the exclusions

The most important protection may be limited by an exclusion, waiting period or condition.

Write down the exclusions that matter to your situation and ask the provider to explain them.
